# Contributing to AiNu PC Hardware Documentation

Thank you for your interest in contributing to our hardware documentation! This repository contains sizing guides, and compliance documentation for on-premises AI deployment.

## What We Accept

- **Hardware compatibility notes** — Experiences with specific models or configurations
- **Compliance updates** — Changes to PDPA/GDPR requirements in Singapore or the EU
- **Documentation improvements** — Clarifications, corrections, better explanations

## What We Don't Accept

- Speculative performance claims without benchmark data
- Vendor-specific marketing content
- Unverified hardware modifications or overclocking guides

## Code of Conduct

- Be respectful and professional
- Provide constructive feedback
- Share credit where due
- Focus on technical merit, not personal opinions
